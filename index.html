Projects

Green FinOps Dashboard

1M1B AI for Sustainability internship, with IBM SkillsBuild and AICTE

Finds idle cloud compute and forgotten storage in telemetry data, then measures the waste in dollars and kilograms of CO₂e and writes plain-English fixes for developers. Carbon figures use the Cloud Carbon Footprint power model, and detection precision and recall are tested against labelled data.

Built with Python, Streamlit, pandas, pytest

Margin, AI Resume Parser

Internship project at Pinnacle Labs

Reads a resume in PDF, DOCX, PNG or JPG, scores it from 0 to 100 and suggests two to four fitting job roles. The result appears as an annotated paper with a hand-drawn score circle and sticky-note comments in the margin. Uploads are size-capped, renamed and deleted right after processing.

Built with React, Vite, FastAPI, Groq API, pdfplumber, python-docx, pytesseract

Heart Disease Classifier

Machine learning classification pipeline

Predicts heart disease risk from 13 clinical attributes of 303 patients in the Kaggle Heart Disease UCI dataset. The pipeline covers cleaning, exploratory analysis, feature engineering and training of Logistic Regression and Decision Tree models tuned with GridSearchCV. Results are judged with a confusion matrix, ROC-AUC, precision, recall and F1-score.

Built with Python, scikit-learn
